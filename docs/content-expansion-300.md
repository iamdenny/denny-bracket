# Content expansion, 24 October 2026

This release adds 100 usable activity presets. Translations do not count as additional activities. Existing routes are retained.

- Spin: 20 contexts, each with sketch, pitch, connection, interview and recall instructions; eight playable results per wheel.
- Type: 20 contexts, each with dialogue, time log, inventory, quotation and handover passages, in four languages.
- Bracket: 25 club contexts with 16/24-entry knockout and 8/12-entry league plans. Court counts and slot lengths are illustrative, not official rules. Tables describe the preset and do not recalculate for edited entries.
- Print: 100 additional arithmetic levels, 24 reproducible questions per edition with correct answers. Existing 100 math IDs retained.
- Daily: 100 five-flag review decks, each testing all five listed flags. Decks revisit the existing 192-country practice pool; no change to daily challenge or streak rules.
- Compare: 100 comparisons across ten SI dimensions with BIPM source links and four-language explanations. Decimal-prefix factors are exact. Tiny values retain significant digits in static tables.
